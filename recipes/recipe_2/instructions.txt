Start with 1/2 cup of pressure-cooked Dal and one tomato.
Finely chop an onion.
Heat 3 teaspoons of oil in a small pot over medium heat for 3 minutes.
Add the chopped onion and reduce the heat to low.
In a small bowl, gather: 1/2 teaspoon chili paste (ginger, garlic, jalapeno), 1/4 teaspoon turmeric, 1/4 teaspoon red chili powder, 1 teaspoon coriander powder, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon Punjabi Masala.
Turn off the stove for 1 minute to cool the oil, then stir in the spices.
Immediately add the prepared Dal.
Turn the heat to medium and add 1/2 teaspoon of lemon juice.
Let the Dal boil for 6 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally.
Turn off the stove and garnish with fresh chopped mint (pudina), if available.
