Wash, peel, and slice the potatoes into long strips (as seen in the picture).
Heat 5 teaspoons of oil in a pan over medium heat.
Add 1/2 teaspoon of cumin (Jeera). Once they crackle, turn off the stove.
Let the oil cool for 2 minutes, then add 1/4 teaspoon turmeric, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon chili paste (ginger, garlic, jalapeno).
Add the potatoes, stir well, and cover with a lid.
After 5 minutes, open the lid, stir, and cover again.
After another 5 minutes, check if the potatoes are softening and adjust salt if needed.
When the potatoes are half-cooked, stir in 1 teaspoon coriander powder and 1/4 teaspoon red chili powder. Cover for 5 minutes.
Once the potatoes are soft, turn off the stove.
Garnish with chopped fresh coriander and 1/2 teaspoon lemon juice, if available.
