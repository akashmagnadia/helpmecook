Peel and slice fresh yam (as seen in the picture) or use a frozen packet.
Heat 4 teaspoons of oil in a pan over medium heat.
Add the yam (frozen or fresh) and cover the pan until it is fully defrosted or begins to soften.
Once defrosted, stir well.
After 2 minutes, stir again.
After another 5 minutes, stir in these spices: 1/2 teaspoon coriander powder, 1/2 teaspoon cumin, and 1/4 teaspoon salt.
Mix well, cover, and cook until finished.
