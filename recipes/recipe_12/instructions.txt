In a stackable container, combine 1/2 cup washed Basmati rice, 1 ⅓ cups water, 1/2 teaspoon cumin, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and 2 teaspoons of Ghee.
Place the stackable container in an Instant Pot, using a trivet to keep it elevated (especially if cooking Dal concurrently at the bottom).
Follow the Instant Pot settings appropriate for your meal combinations.
