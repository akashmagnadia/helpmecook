Finely blend 1 medium onion and 1/2 medium pepper in a mixer.
Heat oil in a large pan and sauté the blended onion-pepper mixture.
Dice 3 medium tomatoes and set aside.
Stir these spices into the pan: 1 teaspoon green chili paste, 1/4 teaspoon turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon red chili spice, 1 teaspoon coriander powder, 1/4 teaspoon Garam Masala, and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
Add the diced tomatoes and cook over low heat, stirring until the moisture has evaporated.
Stir in 4 eggs or shredded Paneer.
Garnish with fresh cilantro before serving.
