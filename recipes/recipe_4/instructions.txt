Wash and dry the okra thoroughly on a napkin.
Wash and cut the potato into pieces (as seen in the picture), adding them directly into the pan.
Trim the ends of each okra and cut the middle portion into small pieces, adding them to the pan with the potatoes.
Place the pan on the stove over medium heat and add 5 teaspoons of oil.
Cover the pan with a lid without stirring.
After 5 minutes, open the lid and add 1/4 teaspoon turmeric and 1/4 teaspoon salt.
Stir well and cover again for 10 minutes.
Open the lid, stir well, and cover again.
Once the okra's stickiness is gone, check if the potato is soft and the okra is crisp.
Add: 1/4 teaspoon chili paste (ginger, garlic, jalapeno), 1 teaspoon coriander powder, 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder, and 1/2 teaspoon lemon juice.
Stir in the spices and cover for 5 minutes.
Open the lid, stir one last time, and turn off the stove after 2 minutes.
