Prepare the vegetables: Wash and chop cabbage (avoiding the root) or cauliflower into large pieces. Peel and cube the potatoes.
Heat 5 teaspoons of oil in a pan over medium heat.
Add mustard seeds. Once they crackle, turn off the stove and let the oil cool for 2 minutes.
Stir in: 1/4 teaspoon turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon chili paste (ginger, garlic, jalapeno).
Add the prepared vegetables, mix well, and return the pan to medium heat.
Cover with a lid for 7 minutes, then stir and cover again.
After 5 minutes, check the potatoes for softness and adjust salt if needed.
When the potatoes are half-cooked, add: 1 teaspoon coriander powder, 1/4 teaspoon red chili powder, and 1/4 teaspoon Pav Bhaji masala.
Top with one medium chopped tomato and fresh coriander (if available), but do not stir yet.
Cook for 3 minutes, then stir everything together.
Turn off the stove after 2 minutes once everything is fully cooked.
