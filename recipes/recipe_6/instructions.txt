Soak 1 cup of pinto beans in water inside an Instant Pot for 6 hours.
Drain the water and return the beans to the Instant Pot with 2 cups of fresh water.
Finely chop 1 small onion and half a jalapeno, and add them to the pot.
Stir in 1/2 teaspoon salt.
Set the Instant Pot to high pressure for 20 minutes.
Once cooked, stir in 3 teaspoons of Taco Seasoning.
Switch to the "Sauté" setting and cook for 5 minutes. Adjust salt if needed.
