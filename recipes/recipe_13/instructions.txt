Prepare the tofu: Drain thoroughly and pat remarkably dry with a paper napkin. Cut into cubes or rectangles.
Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a large flat pan over medium heat.
Fry the tofu until the outside is slightly crisp while the middle remains soft, turning occasionally.
Season the tofu with 1 teaspoon of salt.
While the tofu cooks, dice 1 pepper and 1 onion into long pieces.
Once the tofu is cooked, remove it from the pan. Toss in the vegetables and a handful of spring onions.
Prepare the sauce in a small bowl: 1.5 teaspoons green chili paste, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, 3.5 tablespoons red chili sauce, 3 spoons ketchup, 2 teaspoons sugar, juice of 1/2 a lime, and 1 teaspoon red chili powder.
Use 1 tablespoon of water to rinse the sauce bowl and add it to the pan.
