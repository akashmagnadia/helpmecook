Gather these ingredients: 2 teaspoons all-purpose flour, 1/4 teaspoon red chili powder, 1/4 teaspoon black pepper, 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder, 1/4 teaspoon onion powder, 1/4 teaspoon cumin, 1/4 teaspoon oregano, 1 teaspoon sugar, and 1/4 teaspoon salt.
Open a 14 oz can of diced tomatoes.
Heat 3 teaspoons of oil in a small pan over medium heat.
Sauté only the all-purpose flour for 30 seconds.
Add the remaining spices and cook for another 20 seconds.
Pour in the canned diced tomatoes and 1/2 cup water. Turn off the stove.
Use a hand blender to blend the mixture until smooth.
Turn the heat back to medium.
Stir occasionally to prevent the flour from burning until the sauce thickens and begins to boil.
