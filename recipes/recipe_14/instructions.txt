Place about 12 small potatoes in an Instant Pot with enough water to cover them.
Cook on low heat for 20 minutes.
Once cooked, peel and cut the potatoes into bite-sized pieces.
Heat 3 teaspoons of oil in a large flat pan.
Add 1/2 teaspoon of sesame seeds. Once they begin to bubble, add the potatoes.
Stir in 1/4 teaspoon turmeric and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Let it cook for 10 minutes.
Add 1.5 teaspoons coriander powder, 1 teaspoon red chili spice, and 1/4 teaspoon black salt.
Stir in the juice from 1 lemon.
Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the potatoes are soft and slightly crispy. Adjust spice levels as desired.
