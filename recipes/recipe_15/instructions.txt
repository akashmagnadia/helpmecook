Combine 2 cups of sugar and 1 ⅔ cups of water in a medium saucepan.
Bring to a boil, then remove from heat and let it cool to room temperature.
Peel 5 medium pieces of ginger, chop them finely, and crush them using a mixer.
Mix the cooled sugar syrup with the crushed ginger and the following: 1 cup lemon juice, 1 teaspoon black salt, 1 teaspoon salt, 1/2 teaspoon black pepper, and 4 teaspoons cumin powder.
