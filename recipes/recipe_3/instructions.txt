Heat 3 teaspoons of oil in a small pot over medium heat for 3 minutes.
Add 1/4 teaspoon of mustard seeds (Raay). Once they start to crackle, turn off the stove and let it cool for 2 minutes.
Stir in a pinch of Asafoetida (Hing) and 1/4 teaspoon of red chili powder.
In a small bowl, gather: 1/2 teaspoon chili paste (ginger, garlic, jalapeno), 1/4 teaspoon turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon salt, 1/4 teaspoon Sambhar Masala, 4 teaspoons sugar, and 10 peanuts.
Add the prepared Dal and the gathered spices to the pot.
Stir in 1/2 teaspoon of lemon juice.
Heat over medium for 6 to 7 minutes, stirring every 2 to 3 minutes.
Turn off the stove and garnish with fresh chopped coriander, if available.
