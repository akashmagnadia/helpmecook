Boil water in a tall pan with 1 teaspoon salt and 1/2 tablespoon oil. Add 1 cup of pasta and cook until done.
While the pasta cooks, heat a large pan over medium heat.
Finely mince 4 garlic cloves and chop 1 medium onion.
Melt 1/4 cup butter in the pan and sauté the garlic until aromatic.
Add 4 tablespoons olive oil and the chopped onion.
Chop and add these vegetables sequentially to ensure even cooking: 1 jalapeno, 1 medium bell pepper, 1 large zucchini, and 1 large head of broccoli. (Tip: You can also blanch the broccoli in the pasta water for 1 minute after removing the pasta).
Once the vegetables are tender, stir in 1/2 bottle Alfredo sauce, 1/2 bottle Marinara sauce, and tomato pesto.
Garnish with red pepper flakes and cheese, if desired.
