In a stackable container, combine ⅓ cup Toor Dal, ⅔ cup water, and half a tomato (sliced lengthwise).
In a second stackable container, add 1/2 cup Basmati Rice, 1 ⅓ cups water, and 1/4 tablespoon salt.
Pour 1/2 cup water and two drops of lemon juice into the Instant Pot.
Place the containers inside the Instant Pot, with the Toor Dal container at the bottom and the rice on top.
Close the lid and cook for 20 minutes on low pressure (using the "Beans" setting).
Once the pressure is released, open the lid and remove both containers.
Peel and remove the tomato skin while it's still warm.
Use a handheld mixer to blend the Toor Dal into a smooth gravy.
Stir in 1 ⅓ cups of water.
This base can now be used for three types of Dal: Jeera Dal, Garam Masala Dal, or Khati Meethi Dal.
