Soak 1 cup of Toor Dal in water for 6 hours.
Drain the water and place the dal in an Instant Pot with 2.5 cups of fresh water and 1 teaspoon of Ghee.
(Optional) Follow the Jeera Rice recipe to cook rice alongside the Dal.
Set the Instant Pot to low pressure for 20 minutes.
Once cooked and the pressure is released, garnish with fresh coriander, if available.
