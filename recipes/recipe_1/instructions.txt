Heat 3 teaspoons of oil in a small pot over medium heat for about 3 minutes.
Add 1/2 teaspoon of cumin (Jeera) and reduce the heat to low.
In a small bowl, gather: 1/2 teaspoon chili paste (ginger, garlic, jalapeno), 1/4 teaspoon turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder, 1 teaspoon coriander powder, and 1/2 teaspoon salt. (Optional: Add 1/4 teaspoon Punjabi Masala for Garam Masala Dal).
Turn off the stove for 1 minute to cool the oil, then add the spices.
Immediately stir in the prepared Dal.
Turn the heat back to medium and add 1/2 teaspoon of lemon juice.
Let the Dal boil for 6 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally.
Turn off the stove and garnish with fresh chopped coriander, if available.
