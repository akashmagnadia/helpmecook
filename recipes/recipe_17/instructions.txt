Dice 3 medium tomatoes and set aside.
Heat oil in a pan.
Mix and stir these spices into the oil: 1 teaspoon green chili paste, 1/4 teaspoon turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon red chili spice, 1 teaspoon coriander powder, 1 teaspoon cumin powder, and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
Add the diced tomatoes and cook over medium heat until heated through.
Garnish with sev immediately before serving.
